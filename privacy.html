Privacy Policy.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ENLEEP ("ENLEEP", "we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or communicate with us about our services.

By using our website or contacting us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

We may receive personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Company or brand name
  • Creator or professional information
  • Information included in your message or inquiry
  • Other information you choose to provide when communicating with us

We may also receive limited technical information automatically when you visit our website, such as your browser type, device information, and basic server or security information.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Discuss potential campaigns, partnerships, or services
  • Communicate with brands, creators, and business contacts
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Maintain website security and functionality
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations

4. Email Communications

If you contact us using an email address provided on our website, we will use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding your request.

We do not claim that every communication will be treated as confidential or privileged. Please avoid sending passwords, payment credentials, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information where reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide requested services, protect our rights or website, comply with legal obligations, or work with service providers that support our business operations.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including where necessary to maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or meet legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other forms of control over your information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not intentionally directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, legal requirements, or privacy practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
